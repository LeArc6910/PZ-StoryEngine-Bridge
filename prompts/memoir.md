A survivor in Project Zomboid (Knox County, Kentucky, 1993, after the Knox Event outbreak) has just died. You are given the record of their days and their own diary excerpts. Write a short retrospective of their life after the outbreak, as if written by someone who later found their diary.

Rules:
- Write in the language named on the "Language" line, in the third person.
- Use only what the record says. Do not invent people, places or events. Quote or paraphrase their diary where it helps.
- Follow the arc of their days: where they settled, who they were with, the hard days and the quiet ones.
- End with their death as the record describes it. If the cause is unclear, say so plainly rather than inventing one.
- Eight to fourteen sentences. Plain text only: no title, no lists, no markdown.

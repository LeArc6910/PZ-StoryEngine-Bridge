A survivor in Project Zomboid (Knox County, Kentucky, 1993, after the Knox Event outbreak) has just died. You are given the record of their days, their own diary excerpts and some of their radio conversations. Write a short memoir of their life after the outbreak, as if written by someone who later found their diary and radio log.

Rules:
- Write in the language named on the "Language" line, in the third person.
- Use only what the record says. Do not invent people, places, conversations or events.
- Do not walk through the days one by one. Pick the three or four moments that say the most about who they were (a first fight, a friendship, a promise kept or broken, a quiet day, a hard choice) and tell those as small scenes. Let the rest pass in a line.
- When radio conversations are given, use what was actually said: what they asked for, what they promised, how the people on the other end talked to them. Paraphrase in your own words; you may quote a short phrase of theirs.
- No clock times, no counts of kills or zombies, no day numbers, and never phrases like "according to the record" or "the diary says". Write it as a story, not a report.
- Their death: say where and how as the record describes it, briefly. If the cause is unclear, say so plainly rather than inventing one.
- End with who they were: one or two sentences about the kind of person the record shows.
- Eight to twelve sentences. Plain text only: no title, no lists, no markdown.
